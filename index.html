Biography

About

Tendai Makaripe is a decorated Zimbabwean journalist, academic researcher and media trainer with a strong background in journalism, teaching and research. He is currently the Managing Editor of The Narrative Network (TNN). He is also the founder of Children’s Voices, an online platform focusing on children’s issues, and has earned nearly 20 awards for excellence in journalism across diverse areas such as environmental reporting and human rights-based education.

Academically, he holds a Master’s degree and a Bachelor’s degree in Political Science from the University of Zimbabwe, as well as a Diploma in Journalism and Communication.

As a lecturer, Tendai has taught subjects ranging from Mass Communication Research to International Affairs. His research, often focused on media, politics and governance in Africa, has enriched scholarly discussion in these fields.

Beyond the newsroom, Tendai is the author of Bleeding Hearts, a relationship guide for young people, and has edited several notable books, including The Cactus Creed.
